The Long Road

Turn 65, and the rest of your life runs along one of a few health paths: staying well, living with chronic illness, needing help with daily tasks, needing long-term care. Each path costs Medicare and costs you differently. Pick where you start and see where the road tends to go, what it costs, how bad the worst stretches get, and what happens if Medicare's hospital fund runs short in 2033.

4. Does the model get mortality right?

Life expectancy at 65. The fitted model pools deaths from 1998 to 2022, when mortality was higher than in 2023, so it runs a year or more short. The calculator scales death rates by age and sex to match the 2023 US period life table, keeping each health state's relative risk, and also shows that each state's share of the population by age matches the survey.
